About Me

I am a master's student in Mathematics at UESTC. My current work focuses on mathematical questions arising in generative modeling and scientific computing, especially the design and analysis of numerical methods for diffusion-based models.

Beyond theoretical research, I enjoy building interactive software and translating mathematical and algorithmic ideas into complete computational systems.

Research Interests

  • Mathematical foundations of machine learning
  • Generative models, diffusion processes, and sampling algorithms
  • Numerical analysis, operator splitting, and error analysis
  • Optimization and approximation theory

Generative Modeling & Numerical Sampling

I study numerical methods for diffusion-model sampling, with emphasis on operator splitting, convergence analysis, and quantitative error bounds.

Medical Image Analysis

I designed and implemented a multimodal neural network for breast tumor prediction and classification, including network design, model training, and experimental evaluation.

Peiyi Liu, Zhaoqiang Liu, Yiqi Gu · 2026

We develop operator-splitting-based samplers for diffusion models and analyze their convergence, including second-order accuracy and non-asymptotic error bounds.
